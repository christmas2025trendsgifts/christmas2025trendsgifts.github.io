Unveiling the Magic: Top Destinations for Jet2 Xmas Markets 2025

Jet2’s 2025 programme is set to feature an impressive array of destinations, each offering its unique brand of festive cheer. While the full list will be unveiled closer to the booking period, here are some perennial favourites and exciting possibilities that embody the spirit of the European Christmas market:

Germany: The Heart of Christmas Tradition
Germany is synonymous with Christmas markets, and Jet2 offers fantastic access to some of its most iconic.

  • Cologne: Dominated by its magnificent Gothic cathedral, Cologne boasts several distinct markets. The market at the Cathedral itself is breathtaking, while the Harbour Christmas Market offers a maritime theme, and the Old Town market transports you back in time. Expect traditional wooden stalls, handcrafted gifts, and the rich aroma of roasted almonds and spiced wine.
  • Düsseldorf: Known for its elegant shopping streets, Düsseldorf transforms into a winter wonderland with multiple themed markets spread across its city centre. From the traditional "Markt der Engel" (Angel Market) to the charming "Handwerker-Markt" (Craftsmen’s Market), there’s a unique atmosphere to discover around every corner.
  • Berlin: Germany’s vibrant capital hosts over 80 Christmas markets, ranging from the grand and traditional to the quirky and contemporary. The Gendarmenmarkt market, set against the backdrop of the Konzerthaus and French and German Cathedrals, is particularly renowned for its artisan crafts and gourmet food. The Charlottenburg Palace market offers a regal setting, while Spandau’s market is one of the largest in the region.

Austria: Alpine Charm and Imperial Grandeur
Austria’s markets blend imperial elegance with cozy alpine warmth.

  • Vienna: The Austrian capital offers a truly majestic Christmas experience. The "Christkindlmarkt" on Rathausplatz (City Hall Square) is arguably the most famous, with its towering Christmas tree and festive village. Schönbrunn Palace and Belvedere Palace also host enchanting markets, providing a regal backdrop to your festive shopping.
  • Salzburg: The birthplace of Mozart, Salzburg’s markets are incredibly picturesque, set against the backdrop of its historic fortress and baroque architecture. The "Christkindlmarkt" on Cathedral Square and Residenz Square is a must-visit, offering a traditional atmosphere and exquisite local crafts.

Czech Republic: Bohemian Rhapsody of Lights

  • Prague: The Czech capital is utterly magical during the festive season. The main markets in Old Town Square and Wenceslas Square are famous for their stunning Christmas trees, live nativity scenes, and a vibrant array of stalls selling traditional Czech treats like Trdelník (sweet pastry) and Punch. The city’s gothic architecture, dusted with snow, provides a fairytale setting.

Hungary: A Festive Feast on the Danube

  • Budapest: The markets in Budapest offer a unique blend of Hungarian traditions and festive cheer. The market at Vörösmarty Square is one of the oldest and most beautiful, known for its high-quality handcrafted goods and delicious street food. The market at St. Stephen’s Basilica, with its impressive light show projected onto the basilica’s facade, is another highlight.

Belgium: Chocolate, Waffles, and Winter Wonders

  • Brussels: The "Winter Wonders" festival in Brussels transforms the city into a festive spectacle. Centred around the Grand Place, with its magnificent light and sound show, the market extends through the city, offering ice skating, a Ferris wheel, and countless stalls selling Belgian chocolates, waffles, and local beers.
  • Bruges: The medieval city of Bruges is exceptionally charming at Christmas. Its market on the Markt Square, surrounded by historic guild houses, offers a more intimate and traditional experience, perfect for those seeking a romantic festive getaway.

What to Expect at a European Christmas Market

Beyond the specific locations, the essence of the Christmas market experience remains consistent:

  • Culinary Delights: Prepare your taste buds for an adventure! From classic Glühwein (mulled wine) and Eierpunsch (egg punch) to Bratwurst, roasted chestnuts, gingerbread, and regional specialities, the food stalls are a major draw.
  • Handcrafted Treasures: Discover unique gifts that you won’t find on the high street. Artisans sell everything from intricate wooden ornaments and delicate glass baubles to handmade jewellery, candles, and traditional toys.
  • Enchanting Atmosphere: The air buzzes with excitement. Carol singers, brass bands, and festive music create a joyous soundtrack. The scent of pine, cinnamon, and roasting nuts fills the air, and the twinkling lights cast a warm, inviting glow over everything.
  • Festive Activities: Many markets offer more than just stalls. Look out for ice skating rinks, Ferris wheels, Santa’s grottos, and even live performances that add to the festive fun for all ages.
