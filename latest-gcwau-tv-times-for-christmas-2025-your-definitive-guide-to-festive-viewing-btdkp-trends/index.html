Channel 5: Classic Films and Comfort Viewing
Channel 5 often leans into comfort viewing, with a strong emphasis on classic films, heartwarming documentaries, and factual entertainment. Key Programming Themes and Categories for Christmas 2025

Christmas Day Highlights: The undisputed peak of festive viewing. Expect a carefully curated schedule designed to keep families entertained from morning until night. This will include the Royal Message, a major film premiere, a flagship drama special, and the much-anticipated soap opera showdowns.

Family Favourites: Beyond the main event, expect a continuous stream of animated classics, family-friendly films, and light entertainment shows that appeal across generations. These are the shows that provide the comforting backdrop to gift-opening, festive meals, and board games.

Drama and Mystery: Christmas is a prime time for gripping dramas. Whether it’s a standalone whodunit, a period drama special, or a continuation of a popular series, broadcasters will be vying to capture audiences with compelling storylines and high production values.

Comedy Gold: Laughter is essential at Christmas. Expect festive editions of beloved sitcoms, stand-up specials from top comedians, and perhaps a new comedy-drama designed to bring warmth and humour to the darkest days of winter.

Soaps: The Annual Rollercoaster: EastEnders, Coronation Street, and Emmerdale are guaranteed to deliver their annual dose of high drama, shocking revelations, and emotional twists. These episodes are often the most talked-about of the year, providing a communal viewing experience.

Movies: Blockbusters and Beloved Classics: The film schedule will be packed. Expect recent cinematic hits making their TV debut, alongside a healthy dose of festive classics like Home Alone, Elf, Love Actually, and It’s a Wonderful Life. These films are essential for creating that warm, nostalgic Christmas feeling.

Children’s Programming: Crucial for families, the morning and early afternoon slots will be filled with dedicated children’s programming, from animated specials to festive editions of popular kids’ shows, ensuring the youngest viewers are well entertained.

Beyond Christmas Day: Boxing Day and New Year’s
The festive viewing doesn’t end on Christmas Day. Boxing Day often features major sporting events (like live football or horse racing), more film premieres, and the start of new drama series designed to carry viewers through to the New Year. New Year’s Eve will be dominated by live music spectaculars and countdown shows, while New Year’s Day often brings another wave of big-budget dramas, films, and perhaps a traditional concert or parade.
