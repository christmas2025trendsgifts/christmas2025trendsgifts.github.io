Spotlight on European Destinations: A All manor of events christmas a yuletide extravaganza like no other Continental Christmas Dream

Shearings Xmas Markets 2025 offers an enticing array of European destinations, each with its distinct charm and festive traditions. Prepare to be transported to a world of enchanting sights, sounds, and aromas.

  • Germany: The Heart of Christmas Markets
    Germany is the undisputed birthplace of the Christmas market, and Shearings brings you to its most iconic celebrations. Imagine the grandeur of Nuremberg’s Christkindlesmarkt, one of the oldest and most famous, where the air is thick with the scent of gingerbread and roasted almonds, and stalls overflow with handcrafted ornaments and traditional wooden toys. Or perhaps the majestic backdrop of Cologne Cathedral as you wander through its various markets, sipping Glühwein and admiring the intricate crafts. Dresden’s Striezelmarkt, Germany’s oldest Christmas market, offers a truly authentic experience, famed for its Stollen cake and unique Pflaumentoffel (prune figures). Each German market offers a unique blend of tradition, culinary delights, and artisanal treasures, promising an unforgettable festive journey.

  • Austria: Imperial Grandeur and Alpine Charm
    Experience the refined elegance of an Austrian Christmas with Shearings. Vienna’s Christkindlmarkt on Rathausplatz transforms the city hall square into a fairytale landscape, offering a sophisticated array of crafts, culinary delights, and even an ice-skating rink. In Salzburg, the birthplace of Mozart, the markets exude a Baroque charm, set against the stunning backdrop of the Hohensalzburg Fortress. Here, you can enjoy traditional Austrian pastries, listen to classical Christmas music, and find exquisite handcrafted gifts that reflect the region’s rich artistic heritage.

  • France: Alsatian Fairytales and Parisian Delights
    Shearings tours to France often focus on the enchanting Alsace region, where German and French cultures beautifully intertwine. Strasbourg, proudly proclaimed the "Capital of Christmas," boasts multiple markets across its historic Grande Île, offering a magical blend of festive lights, traditional wooden chalets, and delicious Alsatian specialities like bredele cookies and tarte flambée. Nearby Colmar is a true picture-postcard town, its half-timbered houses providing a stunning backdrop to its five distinct markets, each with its own character. For those seeking urban sophistication, selected tours might also touch upon the smaller, yet charming, markets that spring up across Paris, offering a chic French twist on festive shopping.

  • Belgium: Chocolate, Waffles, and Medieval Magic
    Indulge your senses in Belgium’s delightful Christmas markets. Brussels’ Winter Wonders is a spectacular event, featuring a magnificent Christmas tree, an ice rink, and hundreds of chalets stretching across the Grand Place and beyond, offering Belgian chocolates, waffles, and unique gifts. The medieval charm of Bruges creates an incredibly romantic setting for its Christmas market, with twinkling lights reflecting in the canals and the scent of warm chocolate filling the air. It’s a truly idyllic escape, perfect for couples and those seeking a picturesque festive experience.

Spotlight on UK Destinations: Homegrown Festive Wonders

You don’t have to cross the channel to experience the magic of Christmas markets. Shearings Xmas Markets 2025 also offers a fantastic selection of tours to some of the UK’s most beloved festive destinations, each with its unique character and charm.

  • Birmingham: The Frankfurt Christmas Market
    Often cited as the largest authentic German Christmas market outside of Germany and Austria, Birmingham’s Frankfurt Christmas Market is a must-visit. Shearings tours to Birmingham allow you to immerse yourself in this vibrant market, complete with traditional German stalls selling everything from Bratwurst and Glühwein to handcrafted gifts and festive decorations. The city’s contemporary architecture provides an interesting contrast to the traditional market, creating a unique urban festive experience.

  • Manchester: A City of Festive Lights
    Manchester’s Christmas Markets are renowned for their sheer scale and diverse offerings, spreading across multiple sites throughout the city centre. From the main hub in Albert Square to the artisan crafts in St Ann’s Square and the family-friendly attractions in Cathedral Gardens, there’s something for everyone. Shearings tours to Manchester offer ample time to explore these bustling markets, enjoy local street food, and soak up the lively atmosphere.

  • Edinburgh: A Scottish Winter Wonderland
    Experience a truly magical Christmas in Scotland’s capital. Edinburgh’s Christmas transforms the city into a festive wonderland, with markets stretching from East Princes Street Gardens to the Mound. Against the dramatic backdrop of Edinburgh Castle, you can browse Scottish crafts, enjoy traditional food and drink, and even take a spin on the Ferris wheel for breathtaking views of the illuminated city. Shearings tours to Edinburgh capture the unique charm of a Scottish Christmas.

  • Bath: Georgian Elegance and Artisan Crafts
    The Bath Christmas Market is consistently voted one of the UK’s favourites, and for good reason. Set against the stunning backdrop of Bath Abbey and the Roman Baths, over 150 chalets showcase the best of local and regional artisan products. Shearings tours to Bath offer a chance to find unique, handcrafted gifts, enjoy festive food, and explore the beautiful Georgian city, perhaps even indulging in a warming dip at the Thermae Bath Spa.

  • York: St Nicholas Fair
    Step back in time with a Shearings tour to York’s St Nicholas Fair. The historic streets of York, particularly the Shambles, provide an atmospheric setting for this traditional market. You’ll find local produce, artisan crafts, and plenty of festive cheer, all within the charming confines of one of England’s most historic cities.
